# Privacy Policy — Universe Medical Center

**Last updated: January 2026**

Universe Medical Center ("Universe Clinic", "we", "us") is an internal-medicine clinic with two branches: our Addis Ababa branch on the Ayat-Bole Arabsa Main Road (Lemi Kura Subcity, Woreda 03, Zone 05, Straight 08, Addis Ababa) and our Ambo branch at Heran Clinic, Ambo City, West Shoa, Oromia Region. This website exists so you can learn about our services and reach us easily. Because anything touching on your health is personal, we have kept this policy short, plain, and honest: it explains what this website collects, why, how we protect it, and what you can ask us to do about it.

## What we collect

We collect only what you type into our forms:

- **Contact form** — your name, phone number, email address, the department your message concerns, and the message itself.
- **Appointment request form** — the same details as the contact form, plus your preferred date and time and the service you are interested in.
- **Newsletter** — your email address, nothing more.

We do not ask for payment details on this website, and we do not gather information about you from other websites or apps.

## Why we collect it

For three simple reasons:

1. **To respond to your enquiries.** If you send us a message, we need your name and contact details to reply.
2. **To schedule appointments.** Your preferred date, time, and service help our reception team confirm a slot that works for you.
3. **To improve our services.** Reading messages in aggregate helps us understand what patients ask for most, so we can improve our services.

## We only collect what we need

If a piece of information is not necessary to answer your question or book your appointment, we do not ask for it. Our forms have no required fields that exist purely for marketing.

## Health information is sensitive

We treat anything you share about your health as confidential and sensitive. It is read only by the staff who need it to help you, and it is never used for advertising or profiling. One practical request: please avoid sending detailed medical histories through the website — call us on 0116390354 or speak with a doctor in person instead.

## We never sell your personal data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information with anyone, and we never will. Your details are used to serve you, not to market to you.

## How your data is stored

Information submitted through this website is stored securely, with access limited to authorized clinic staff who need it to do their work. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, and we keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required for clinic records.

## Cookies

This website uses only the basic cookies required for the site to function — for example, remembering your language preference or keeping a signed-in administrator's session. We do not use advertising cookies, and we do not follow you across other websites. You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; the site will still work.

## Third parties

We do not share your personal data with third parties, except for the hosting and email providers we rely on to run this website and deliver your messages to us. They process data only as needed to provide those services, never for their own purposes.

## Your rights

You may ask us at any time to:

- show you the personal data we hold about you;
- correct anything that is inaccurate; or
- delete your data.

Email **universeclinic2023@gmail.com** and we will respond within a reasonable time. Newsletter messages always include an unsubscribe link, so you can opt out at any time.

## Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Appointments for a child should be requested by a parent or guardian. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

## Medical disclaimer

Information on this site is educational and does not replace professional medical advice. In an emergency, call 0116390354 or visit the clinic immediately.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the website or our practices evolve. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date, so please check back occasionally.
